# data/ — auto-refreshed vulnerability feeds

Files here are **generated by the pipeline** (`feeds/update_feeds.py`), not
edited by hand:

- `kev.json` — CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities (list of actively-exploited CVE ids)
- `nvd_recent.json` — recently-modified CVEs from NVD with CVSS/severity
- `manifest.json` — last-update timestamps and counts

`cve_intel.py` reads these first (so enrichment works even offline). They are
kept current by:
- the **GitHub Actions** workflow `.github/workflows/update-feeds.yml` (runs in
  the cloud on a schedule — works even when your machine is off), and/or
- a local **systemd timer / cron** installed via `deploy/install-scheduler.sh`.
